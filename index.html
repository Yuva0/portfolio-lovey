Frequently Asked Questions

At what age should I seek speech therapy for my child?

There is no age too early. If your child is not babbling by 12 months, has no words by 18 months, or is hard to understand by age 3, an assessment is worthwhile. Early support is consistently the most effective support.

Are online sessions as effective as in-person therapy?

Yes. Research on teletherapy shows outcomes comparable to in-person sessions for most goals. Online sessions also make it easier for parents to observe and carry techniques into daily routines at home.

How long is each session and how often are they?

Sessions run 45–60 minutes. Most clients start with 2 sessions a week, adjusted after the initial assessment based on goals and progress.

Do parents need to attend the sessions?

For young children, yes — parent involvement is a large part of why therapy works. You will get home activities and coaching so progress continues between sessions.

Do you work with adults?

Yes. I work with adults on voice disorders, fluency, aphasia after stroke, cognitive-communication difficulties, and professional communication skills.

Which languages do you offer therapy in?

English and Hindi, and therapy plans are built around your family's home language so learning transfers naturally to everyday life.
